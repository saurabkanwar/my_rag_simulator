💡 What is RAG?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation helps an LLM answer using relevant information from documents, policies, FAQs and more.

Why it matters

✓Uses your organisation's information
✓Can use updated documents
✓Helps ground answers in evidence
✓Does not guarantee correctness

Retrieve relevant context

Search compares the query with indexed chunks, then ranks and selects candidates.

Top-k
Prepare documents, then search to retrieve relevant chunks.
What to notice: Retrieval finds candidate evidence. A relevance score is a ranking signal—not a probability that the answer is true.
